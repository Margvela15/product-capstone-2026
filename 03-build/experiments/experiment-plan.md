# 1. Hypothesis

We believe KIU dorm students experience frustration and passive conflict because shared chores and responsibilities are not tracked clearly. If we provide a simple shared dashboard with task assignment and completion tracking, at least 30% of invited students will actively create or join a dorm group within the first week because they want fair responsibility sharing and fewer roommate conflicts.

---

# 2. Assumption Being Tested

The biggest assumption is that KIU dorm students are willing to actively use a shared digital system to manage chores instead of handling responsibilities informally through chat or verbal agreements.

---

# 3. Top 3 Riskiest Assumptions

| Rank | Assumption | Why risky | Why this experiment addresses it |
|------|------------|----------|----------------------------------|
| 1 | Students will regularly use a shared task dashboard | Users may ignore the app after initial signup | The experiment measures real joins and activity |
| 2 | Students care enough about fairness to adopt the system | Problem may not feel painful enough | User actions show actual demand |
| 3 | Shared tracking reduces roommate tension | Users may still argue despite visibility | Follow-up feedback checks perceived usefulness |

---

# 4. Experiment Method

## Method Chosen
Prototype test

## Exact Setup

- Channel:
  KIU dorm student WhatsApp groups, Instagram stories, and direct invitations

- Asset used:
  Clickable Figma prototype showing dorm creation, task assignment, and completion tracking

- Call to action:
  "Join DormSync and organize your dorm chores fairly"

- Real target users are reached by:
  Sharing the prototype with actual KIU dorm students

- What happens after a user responds:
  Users test the prototype and complete a short form about whether they would realistically use the system

---

# 5. Success, Gray Zone, Failure

- Success threshold:
  30% or more of invited students create or join a dorm group

- Gray zone:
  Between 15% and 29%

- Failure threshold:
  Below 15%

These thresholds are frozen before launch and will not change after data collection begins.

---

# 6. Time Window and Sample Size

- Experiment starts:
  May 9, 2026

- Experiment ends:
  May 16, 2026

- Minimum sample target:
  30 KIU dorm students

- What counts as one valid data point:
  A student who fully interacts with the prototype and submits the response form

# 9. Decision Rule

- If result meets success threshold, we will:
  Continue Sprint 1 implementation and prioritize real-time shared task management features.

- If result falls below failure threshold, we will:
  Reconsider the problem scope and simplify the product toward reminder-only functionality.

---

# 10. What Would Make This Experiment Invalid

- Testing mostly on teammates or close friends
- Changing the prototype during the experiment window
- Sharing the prototype only with technical students
- Collecting opinions without measuring actual interaction
