## HEADER SECTION

**Product name:** KIU Nexus

**Tagline:** A single prioritized academic feed for KIU CS students who miss deadlines hidden in Moodle, Teams, Discord, WhatsApp, and Messenger.

---

## PROBLEM SECTION

Third-year KIU CS students with 3+ project courses waste 30–40 minutes daily sweeping Moodle, Teams, Messenger, Discord, and WhatsApp, and still miss grade-costly deadline changes.

---

## SOLUTION SECTION

KIU Nexus aggregates academic updates into one triaged feed so students can acknowledge critical items and add them instantly to a weekly plan.
